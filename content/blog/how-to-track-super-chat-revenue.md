**To track Super Chat revenue, open YouTube Studio → Analytics → Revenue, where
earnings from Supers (Super Chat, Super Stickers and Super Thanks) are broken out
from ad revenue.** Those figures are estimates and arrive with a delay, so if you
want a running total *during* the stream you need something reading your live
chat. Both are covered below, along with what you actually keep from each Super
Chat.

## Where YouTube shows Super Chat revenue

In **YouTube Studio**:

1. Go to **Analytics** and open the **Revenue** tab.
2. Look at the revenue sources breakdown. Super Chat is reported under
   **Supers**, alongside Super Stickers and Super Thanks.
3. To see a single stream, open that video's analytics instead of the channel's.

Two things trip people up:

* **It is an estimate, and it lags.** Studio's revenue figures update after the
  fact and can change until the month is finalised. They are not a live counter.
* **It is already converted.** Studio reports in your payout currency, even
  though viewers paid in their own currency.

## How to track Super Chat revenue during a live stream

Studio cannot tell you, mid-stream, that you have made $140 so far. The options:

| Method | Live? | Effort | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| YouTube Studio Revenue tab | No | None | Estimates, delayed, converted |
| Spreadsheet by hand | Yes | High | Easy to miss one while you play |
| A tool that reads live chat | Yes | Low | Totals as Super Chats arrive |

**By hand** works for small streams: keep a spreadsheet open and add each Super
Chat as it comes in. The problem is that the big ones arrive at the moments you
are busiest.

**A tool that reads your live chat** sees each Super Chat as it is posted and adds
it up for you. That is the only way to get an accurate running total without
looking away from the game.

## What you actually keep from a Super Chat

The amount a viewer pays is not what lands in your account:

* **YouTube's share.** Creators receive 70% of Super Chat revenue after local
  sales tax and, for purchases made in iOS apps, app store fees; YouTube covers
  card processing costs ([YouTube's Super Chat help](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/7288782)).
  Your own agreement in YouTube Studio is the final word.
* **Currency conversion.** Super Chats in other currencies are converted at
  payout, so a live total in the viewer's currency will not match Studio exactly.
* **Taxes.** Depending on where you live, income tax applies on top.

A useful habit: treat any live total as **gross**, and Studio's finalised monthly
number as the one you budget from.

## Make the most of Super Chats once you can see them

Seeing the total live is only useful if it changes what you do:

* **Thank people by name, promptly.** A fast, specific thank-you is what makes
  the next viewer send one.
* **Mark the moment.** A Super Chat during a big play is a clip waiting to happen.
* **Give it a moment on stream.** Some streamers cut to a celebration scene for
  larger Super Chats — see [how to switch OBS scenes from chat activity](/blog/auto-switch-obs-scenes-guide).
* **Use the numbers with sponsors.** Engaged, paying viewers are exactly what a
  sponsor wants to hear about; [how to price a sponsorship](/blog/how-much-to-charge-for-stream-sponsorships)
  covers how to turn your numbers into a rate.

## Doing it automatically

If you want the live total without the spreadsheet, streamerOS reads your YouTube
chat on your own PC — no API key — and totals Super Chat revenue as it arrives,
per currency, on its [Live Cockpit](/features/live-cockpit). It can also switch
your OBS scene when a Super Chat crosses an amount you set. It is a Windows app
launching in February 2027, $29 once after a free trial.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**How do I see how much I made from Super Chats on one stream?**
Open that stream's video in YouTube Studio, go to its analytics, and check the
Revenue tab. The figure is an estimate and may take a day or more to appear.

**How much does YouTube take from Super Chats?**
Creators receive 70% of Super Chat revenue after local sales tax and, for iOS
purchases, app store fees.

**Why doesn't my live Super Chat total match YouTube Studio?**
Live totals are gross amounts in the viewers' currencies. Studio reports your
share after YouTube's cut and fees, converted to your payout currency.
