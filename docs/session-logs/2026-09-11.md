should become a selling point, then update the India page and FAQ.

## 4. Launch blockers found in the product

These make a launch-page claim untrue until fixed:

1. **Shorts Factory cannot encode in the installed app.**
   `services/ffmpeg_orchestrator.rs` looks for `ffmpeg.exe`/`ffprobe.exe` next to
   the executable, but `tauri.conf.json` has no `bundle.externalBin`, so the
   installer ships without them → "ffmpeg binary not found".
2. **Aura Scene overlays cannot reach OBS from the installed app.**
   `services/aura_scene_server.rs` (port 4500) serves `/assets/*` and
   `/api/scene/active` only. The renderer page OBS should load exists only on the
   Next dev server (`localhost:3000/aura-render`); the spec calls production
   serving a "follow-up". (Aura **Studio** overlays are fine — served by the
   vibe server.)
3. **Brand Guard's model is ~142 MB, not ~75 MB** (`model_manager.rs` pins
   `ggml-base.en`). The site now says "about 140 MB"; the product's PRD still says
   ~75 MB.
4. **Second-PC licensing policy is undecided.** A key is bound to one
   Installation ID. The pricing FAQ says "get in touch before you buy" for a
   gaming PC + streaming PC setup — decide the policy and update it.
